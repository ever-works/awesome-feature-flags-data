## Overview

FeatureVisor is a feature management platform designed for developers, emphasizing GitOps workflows. It allows teams to define feature flags and targeting rules in version-controlled files and deploy them consistently across environments.

## Features

- Feature flag management
- GitOps-based workflow
- Version-controlled configuration
- TypeScript SDKs
- Targeting and segmentation
- Rollout management

## Pricing

Free and open-source.
