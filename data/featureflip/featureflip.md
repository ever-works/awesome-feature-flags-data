## Overview

Featureflip is a full-featured feature flag platform that removes its own dead flags by submitting its own pull requests. It is designed for developers who want automated flag lifecycle management.

## Key Features

- Full-featured feature flag management
- Automatic dead flag removal via pull requests
- Free plan includes 10 flags, 2 environments, 1 project, 3 users
- No usage caps

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted managed platform

## Pricing

Free plan available; paid plans for larger teams and higher usage.
