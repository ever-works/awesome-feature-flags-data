## Overview

Toggled.dev is an enterprise-ready, scalable multi-regional feature toggles management platform designed for teams that need reliable feature flag infrastructure across regions.

## Key Features

- Multi-regional feature toggle management
- SDK for application integration
- Analytics dashboard
- Release calendar
- Slack notifications
- Unlimited requests on free plan
- Up to 10 flags, two environments on free plan

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted managed platform

## Pricing

Free plan: up to 10 flags, two environments, unlimited requests, SDK, analytics dashboard, release calendar, Slack notifications.
