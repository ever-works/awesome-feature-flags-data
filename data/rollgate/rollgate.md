## Overview

Rollgate is an EU-hosted feature flag management platform offering scheduled releases, instant rollback, and A/B testing. It includes 12 SDKs for various platforms.

## Key Features

- Scheduled releases
- Instant rollback
- A/B testing
- 12 SDKs included
- EU-hosted for data residency
- Unlimited flags
- 3 team members on free plan
- No credit card required

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (EU)

## Pricing

Free plan: up to 500K API requests/month, unlimited flags, 3 team members, no credit card required.
