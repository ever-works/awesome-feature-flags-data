## Overview

Eppo is a next-generation A/B experimentation platform designed to enable entrepreneurial culture through data-driven experimentation. It provides warehouse-native experimentation capabilities with feature flags, metric tracking, and experiment analysis.

## Features

- A/B testing and experimentation
- Warehouse-native architecture
- Feature flag management
- Metric tracking and analysis
- Experiment design and monitoring
- Statistical significance testing
